# My Favorite Series: The Walking Dead

The Walking Dead is a post-apocalyptic horror television series based on the comic book series created by Robert Kirkman, Tony Moore, and Charlie Adlard.  
It follows a group of survivors trying to stay alive after a zombie apocalypse, while facing not only the undead but also the darker side of humanity.

## Main Characters
- Rick Grimes – A former sheriff’s deputy and the group’s leader.
- Daryl Dixon – A skilled tracker and crossbow expert.
- Michonne– A warrior known for her katana and quiet strength.
- Carol Peletier – One of the longest-surviving characters, known for her resilience.

## Setting
The series begins in **Atlanta, Georgia**, and later explores various survivor communities like:
- Alexandria Safe-Zone
- Hilltop Colony
- The Kingdom
- The Commonwealth

## Why I Love This Series
- Intense storytelling and emotional character development.
- Great mix of survival, action, and moral dilemmas.
- Constantly evolving world and high-stakes drama.

## Favorite Episodes
1. Season 1, Episode 1 – “Days Gone Bye”: Rick wakes up to a new world overrun by the undead.
2. Season 5, Episode 1 – “No Sanctuary”: The group fights back against the cannibals at Terminus.
3. Season 7, Episode 1 – The Day Will Come When You Won’t Be: One of the most emotional and shocking episodes.
